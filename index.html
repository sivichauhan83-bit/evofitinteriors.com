Frequently Asked Questions

Answers before you ask

Straight answers about how an Evofit fit-out works. Anything else, send us a message.

What does turnkey interior fit-out mean?

It means one team looks after the whole project: design and drawings, civil, electrical, HVAC and plumbing work, ceilings, flooring and partitions, procurement, site coordination and the final handover. You work with one accountable team instead of coordinating several contractors yourself.

Which types of spaces do you work on?

Offices, homes and apartments, retail stores, cafés and restaurants, bars and hospitality venues, and commercial spaces such as receptions, lounges and washrooms. We also take up renovation and refurbishment of existing spaces.

Where do you work?

We are based in Faridabad, Haryana, and have delivered projects in Faridabad, Gurugram, New Delhi, Noida and IMT Manesar, with a site currently in progress in Jaipur. Tell us where your project is and we will discuss how we can support you.

How do I get a quote?

Send the enquiry form on this page or message us on WhatsApp with your location, approximate area, timeline and budget range. We will review your requirements and get back to you.

Can you renovate a space that is already in use?

Yes. Renovation and refurbishment work is planned around your operations, so new finishes, lighting and layouts can be introduced in a way that suits how the space is used.

What happens at handover?

The last stage of every project is final quality assurance, snag resolution and a transparent handover, so you receive a finished space you have checked together with us.

Can I see examples of your work?

Yes. The Projects section shows completed and ongoing work with photo galleries. Open the View Project link on any project to browse its photographs.
